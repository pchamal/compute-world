United States: $2.0T of compute running today

United States sits on a resource ceiling of roughly 571 GW, worth $34.3T to $45.7T of AI compute at today's prices, about 2 times its $30.8T GDP. The bankable slice today is $6.4T, which is $18.3k for every citizen, discounted mainly for physical and governance. It has built 18.7% of the ceiling and runs 41.0 GW on a grid of roughly 1300 GW, a Gross Domestic Compute of $2.0T. An incumbent: already priced in, already building.
