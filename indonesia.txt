Indonesia sits on a resource ceiling of roughly 137 GW, worth $8.2T to $10.9T of AI compute at today's prices, about 8 times its $1.4T GDP. The bankable slice today is $1.5T, which is $5.1k for every citizen, discounted mainly for governance and physical. It has built 6.8% of the ceiling and runs about 400 MW on a grid of roughly 90 GW, a Gross Domestic Compute of $20B. Emerging upside: a narrower gap, and a real one.
