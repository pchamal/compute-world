Definitions

CNW Ceiling ($B) = resource ceiling GW × $60–80B per GW (NVIDIA all-in AI-factory figure, reviewed weekly). CNW Unlockable ($B) = firm untapped GW × $50B × Readiness. GDC ($B, Gross Domestic Compute) = live datacenter IT GW × $50B. Realized (0–100) = 35% conversion + 25% pipeline + 25% Wire signal velocity + 15% execution. Readiness (%) = 18% governance + 13% stability + 14% GPU access + 11% grid + 11% fiber + 8% momentum + 14% physical + 11% capital access. Built (%) = installed hydro+geothermal ÷ resource ceiling. Headline finding: global ceiling ~$662T, unlockable ~$64T, GDC ~$4.7T: the world has tapped 0.7% of its compute net worth.
