Fervo signs 396 MW, 15-year Google PPA at Cape Station — foundation for a potential Utah data center

On 1 September 2026 Fervo Energy (Nasdaq: FRVO) announced a 396 MW power purchase agreement with Google for enhanced-geothermal output from the Cape Station GeoCluster in Beaver County, Utah. Fervo's 8-K dates the contract to 26 August 2026 between wholly owned Cape Generating Station 6 LLC and Google Energy LLC: four successive 99 MW sub-tranches with target commercial operation beginning Q3 2028, a 15-year delivery term, and parent guarantees from Fervo and Alphabet. Fervo will offer Google an option to add roughly 600 MW — nearly 1 GW in total — with a guaranteed COD no later than June 2030, and may owe Google reimbursement of certain payments if it fails to make that expansion offer. Google describes the energy as a foundational building block for a potential Utah data center; final data-center plans stay subject to engineering, state and local approvals and commercial conditions, with delivery leaning on Utah SB 132 direct-to-load pathways. Billed as the largest enhanced-geothermal PPA to date.

Jul 14, 2026🇺🇸 Policy · Stall · Energy

Hochul EO 62: New York pauses DEC permits for data centers ≥50 MW until a GEIS lands

On 14 July 2026 Governor Kathy Hochul signed Executive Order No. 62, the first statewide data-center permitting pause. The order recites nearly 12 GW of data-center load in the NYISO interconnection queue as of May 2026, more than 8 GW of it added in 2025. Until DPS files a final Generic Environmental Impact Statement and findings under SEQRA, DEC must hold in abeyance discretionary permits for construction or expansion of a "data center" that were not already deemed complete — defined as a facility that consumes or can consume 50 MW or more, with manufacturing, research (including Empire AI), education, and medical-care sites carved out. Local-government permits are not paused. DPS must run the GEIS through Case 26-E-0045 (Energize NY / large-load interconnection); ESD has 60 days to post a Community Investment Framework; DEC has twelve months to report on water-withdrawal rules. The press release called this a one-year pause; the order itself lasts until the GEIS lands, with no statutory clock. This is a state conversion gate on NYISO-side load, not a campus FID. Dylan Patel pointed at it on 25 August as the policy drag on US supply; the primary is the EO.

How the Signal Score works

Signal Score, 0 to 100, four disclosed components. Source tier, 40%: primary documents (government registers, regulator rulings, company releases) score highest, then wire services and majors, trade press, regional outlets, aggregators. Corroboration, 30%: Confirmed means a primary source plus at least two independents; Corroborated means multiple independents; Single-source and Disputed are labeled as exactly that. Specificity, 20%: announcements that name megawatts, dollars, GPU counts, sites, and dates outrank ones that name a vision. Delivery context, 10%: claims from actors with live or building projects in the precedents table outrank first announcements, and actors with stalled projects are discounted.
