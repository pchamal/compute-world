UK Budget sets a Data Centre Covenant: 6 GW of AI data centre capacity needed by 2030, with grid capacity reserved for strategic sites that sign up

HM Treasury published the Data Centre Covenant with the Autumn Budget on 28 October 2026. The government estimates the UK needs 6 GW of AI data centre capacity by 2030 so it is not reliant almost entirely on overseas compute. Strategic data centres that want enhanced support must meet tests: credible commitments for the grid capacity they request, a Local Benefit Plan scaled to project size, a Power Usage Effectiveness of 1.3 or better with annual energy and water reporting, local jobs and apprenticeships with a contribution to British AI hardware, and secure access to compute for the NHS and public services. In return, ministers will use Planning and Infrastructure Act powers to reserve and reallocate grid capacity, allow transfer of pre-2030 connection contracts and, in narrow cases, temporary on-site generation. The Budget also adds £140 million for the Sovereign AI Unit. Caveat: which sites count as strategic and the full criteria are still to be published, and a draft National Policy Statement goes to consultation. Country conversion: the UK trades grid priority for local and security commitments.
