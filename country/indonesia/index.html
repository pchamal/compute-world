Indonesia compute capacity

Indonesia runs about 400 MW of live datacenter capacity as of 8 September 2026, a Gross Domestic Compute of $20B. Its identified energy resource could host about 137 GW of AI compute, a ceiling worth $8.2 to 10.9T at today's price of a gigawatt, about 8 times its $1,446B GDP. The bankable slice today, discounted mainly for governance and physical conditions, is $1.5T, or $5,107 per person. Indonesia is emerging upside, ranked 10 of 108 countries by unlockable value.

Questions people ask

How much data center capacity does Indonesia have?

Indonesia runs about 400 MW of live datacenter IT capacity as of 8 Sep 2026, compiled from SemiAnalysis, Cushman & Wakefield, Rystad, Knight Frank and national reports. Valued at $50B per gigawatt, that is a Gross Domestic Compute of $20B.

What is Indonesia's compute net worth?

The ceiling is $8.2 to 10.9T: 137 GW of identified hydro, geothermal, solar, wind and surplus fossil resource valued at $60 to 80B per gigawatt, NVIDIA's all-in figure for an AI factory. That is 8 times Indonesia's $1,446B GDP. The bankable slice today, after a readiness discount of 63%, is $1.5T.

What holds Indonesia back from converting its energy into compute?

Readiness scores 63% on an eight-part discount stack (governance, stability, GPU access, grid, fiber, momentum, physical conditions and capital access). For Indonesia the binding discounts are governance and physical conditions. It has built 6.8% of its ceiling as generating capacity, and its conversion score is 31 of 100.

How does Indonesia compare with its neighbours?

Indonesia ranks 10 of 108 countries by unlockable value, and 1 of 10 in SE Asia. The regional leader is Indonesia at $1.5T unlockable and $20B of live compute.
