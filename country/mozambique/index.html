Mozambique compute capacity

Mozambique runs about 5 MW of live datacenter capacity as of 9 September 2026, a Gross Domestic Compute of under $1B (an estimate). Its identified energy resource could host about 62 GW of AI compute, a ceiling worth $3.7 to 5.0T at today's price of a gigawatt, about 226 times its $22B GDP. The bankable slice today, discounted mainly for capital markets and governance, is $507B, or $13,713 per person. Mozambique is a sleeping giant, ranked 35 of 108 countries by unlockable value.

Questions people ask

How much data center capacity does Mozambique have?

Mozambique runs about 5 MW of live datacenter IT capacity as of 9 Sep 2026, an estimate: country totals below the top 35 markets are estimated. Valued at $50B per gigawatt, that is a Gross Domestic Compute of under $1B.

What is Mozambique's compute net worth?

The ceiling is $3.7 to 5.0T: 62 GW of identified hydro, geothermal, solar, wind and surplus fossil resource valued at $60 to 80B per gigawatt, NVIDIA's all-in figure for an AI factory. That is 226 times Mozambique's $22B GDP. The bankable slice today, after a readiness discount of 43%, is $507B.

What holds Mozambique back from converting its energy into compute?

Readiness scores 43% on an eight-part discount stack (governance, stability, GPU access, grid, fiber, momentum, physical conditions and capital access). For Mozambique the binding discounts are capital markets and governance. It has built 3.6% of its ceiling as generating capacity, and its conversion score is 6 of 100.

How does Mozambique compare with its neighbours?

Mozambique ranks 35 of 108 countries by unlockable value, and 10 of 19 in Africa. The regional leader is Ethiopia at $826B unlockable and $1.0B of live compute.
