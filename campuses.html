Named campuses, not a census.

This is a desk-curated named-project register, not a global inventory. Twenty-three pins, each folded from a primary the desk fetched. Primaries that publish a megawatt figure are almost all ≥250 MW announcements. Energized MW is almost never published. A 4.75 GW interconnection is not IT. SSA, South Asia, and APAC-ex-Oceania have no pins in this book — that is a coverage hole, not a load of zero. Homer City Energy Campus is held: it is a generation plant.

How to read this

  • Not a census. Empty regions and empty small-MW filters are coverage holes, not bugs.
  • MW-null / not_disclosed pins stay on the default view. A MW-threshold filter hides them; they cannot pass a numeric floor.
  • Never imply a pin is live load unless status is operational and the primary said so.
  • Do not treat LBNL Queued Up as data-center load. It is generation interconnection.
  • Homer City Energy Campus is held (generation plant). It is not a pin.
