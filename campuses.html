Named campuses, not a census.

This is a desk-curated named-project register, not a global inventory. One hundred pins, each folded from a primary the desk fetched. Virginia is a named-campus shelf in this book, not Data Center Alley. Ireland is a named-campus shelf, not Dublin metro. Malaysia is a named-campus shelf, not a Johor census. Japan is a named-campus shelf, not a Tokyo/Inzai/Osaka census. Honest holes remain: no named AWS Loudoun or AWS Prince William campus (the AWS pins here are Louisa County only), and no named AWS Ireland campus. Google’s city-only “Dublin, Ireland” line is not a pin. Microsoft Southeast Asia 3, Malaysia West, and AWS ap-southeast-5 are cloud regions, not campus pins. Sub-250 disclosed pins are kept; the 250 MW floor is a filter, not a delete. Energized MW is almost never published. A 4.75 GW interconnection is not IT. SSA and South Asia have no pins in this book — that is a coverage hole, not a load of zero. Homer City Energy Campus is held: it is a generation plant. Equinix JH1 / KL1 and TM IPDC / KVDC are held (hall grain).

How to read this

  • Not a census. Empty regions and empty small-MW filters are coverage holes, not bugs.
  • Virginia fold (2026-08-22): nineteen named campuses. This is a named-campus shelf, not Data Center Alley. No named AWS Loudoun or AWS Prince William campus; AWS pins in this book are Louisa County only.
  • Ireland fold (2026-08-22): twelve named campuses. This is a named-campus shelf, not Dublin metro. No named AWS Ireland campus. Google’s city-only “Dublin, Ireland” line is not a pin. Do not copy Meta 723.8 MW, Microsoft 1 GW / 500 MW, or Echelon 1 GW / 800 MW wind — those are generation or PPAs. Microsoft Jigginstown and Herbata share Naas townlands but are different operators; they are not merged.
  • Malaysia fold (2026-08-22): sixteen named campuses. This is a named-campus shelf, not a Johor census. AirTrunk MEL1/MEL2/SYD3 are not re-pinned. Yondr Sedenak is not added (sold to Vantage; same campus as vantage-jhb1-sedenak). Equinix JH2 (unnamed JS-SEZ) is not added. Equinix JH1 / KL1 and TM IPDC / KVDC are held (hall grain — IBX / combined 20 MW IT). Microsoft Southeast Asia 3, Malaysia West, and AWS ap-southeast-5 are cloud regions, not pins.
  • Japan fold (2026-09-04): thirty named campuses (17 second-pass + 7 first-pass restores including Tomakomai + 6 third-pass). This is a named-campus shelf, not a Tokyo/Inzai/Osaka census. Equinix TY12x / TY13x / OS2x are xScale campus pins with named Inzai/Minoh addresses, not IBX halls. Classic IBX halls (TY2/TY3/etc.) are not added. ntt-inzai-shiroi-campus 250 corridor is dropped; TKY11 (50 IT) and TKY12 (200 IT) are the pins. ESR Cosmosquare is 96 MW IT (JV Critical IT Load), not 120 LEED or 130 building load. Do not use AirTrunk’s ~530 MW Japan platform total. STACK Keihanna / KIX01 keeps 72 from the JV PR (later 80 is a conflict, left in notes). SoftBank IDCF Tomakomai is restored (named Tomakomai City; 300 facility power). SoftBank Fuchu is distinct from DayOne/Gaw Fuchu. Colt Keihanna is distinct from ESR-STACK KIX01 and NTT Soraku. Keppel Inzai is one campus pin (not per-building). google-inzai, softbank-tokyo-ariake, equinix-os2x-minoh, and keppel-tokyo-dc-inzai stay MW-null. mcdr-mitaka is not added.
  • Sub-250 disclosed pins are kept. The 250 MW floor is a filter, not a delete.
  • MW-null / not_disclosed pins stay on the default view. A MW-threshold filter hides them; they cannot pass a numeric floor.
  • Never imply a pin is live load unless status is operational and the primary said so.
  • Do not treat LBNL Queued Up as data-center load. It is generation interconnection.
  • Homer City Energy Campus is held (generation plant). It is not a pin.
  • CloudHQ LC is printed as 1,700 MW from “over 1.7 GWs”; the same page also prints 1,256 MW Total Critical IT Load. The conflict is left on the pin.
  • CloudHQ CLP has no MW: the same page prints 215 MW and 142 MW.
  • CleanArc VA1 grain is interconnection (“critical grid capacity”), not IT. EdgeCore Shannon Hill grain is facility power (“over 1.1 gigawatts of power”).
  • Vantage VA1 operational status is kept; the awards line on the location page is a weak operational quote.
  • Compass Digital Gateway is canceled (2026-04-29): “we cannot move forward with the Prince William Digital Gateway project.” No MW.
  • Google Loudoun / Prince William / Chesterfield share the VEDP 2025-08-27 primary. Do not copy Google “over 500 megawatts of new energy capacity” — generation, not campus IT.
  • Vantage DUB1 is 52 MW IT (“The first two phases consist of 52MW of IT capacity”). Do not use 100 MVA generation.
  • Herbata (Naas) is a Kildare grant that was appealed (eplanning APPEALED 16/09/2025). Status stays announced. No official MW.
  • Art Ennis inspector 200 MW is 83 grid + 120 on-site generation. MW stays null; do not display 200 as IT.
  • Red Admiral status is undisclosed (company still “seeking Planning Permission”; ABP appeal 17/06/2026). Do not invent announced.
  • CyrusOne DUB1 is 54 MW IT (“potential to provide a total 54 MW of IT capacity”). Do not use 80 MVA.
  • EdgeConneX Dublin prints 32 / 170 / 18+7+7 / 100 MW on one page. MW is left null; this desk did not pick one.
  • Mayo Data Hub / Killala is a live An Bord Pleanála appeal (322575). Status is undisclosed. Do not copy Orion / 36–60 MW.
  • AirTrunk JHB3 and JHB4 have no per-campus MW: the primary prints a combined 280 MW IT load only. They share the Iskandar Puteri centroid with JHB2 and offset.
  • DayOne NTP and KTP have no MW: the same Johor page prints 390 MW Total Capacity in Service and over 478 MW of operational capacity.
  • Vantage KUL2 has no MW: the groundbreaking page prints 256 MW IT; another print on the same family is 436 MW IT. The conflict is left on the pin.
  • YTL Green Data Center Park is 600 MW existing facility power, not 1,000 MW planned, not solar.
  • TM Nxera is 200 MW scalable facility power. Do not use 280 TNB ESA.
  • Bridge MY07 is 200 MW IT. Do not use a later 400.
  • PDG JH1 is 200 MW, not 150/52. STACK Iskandar Puteri is 220 MW; do not collapse to 216.
  • Sub-250 disclosed Malaysia pins stay: Vantage KUL1 31 MW IT, NEXTDC KL1 65 MW IT.
  • Equinix JH1 (Nusajaya) and KL1 (Cyberjaya) are held: hall grain (500 cabinets / 1,800 m² and 900 cabinets / 2,630 m²), same as Equinix DB7x. TM IPDC and KVDC are held: those two only have a combined 20 MW IT.

  • Equinix TY12x (54 IT), TY13x (36 facility), and OS2x (MW-null) are xScale campus pins. Do not add classic IBX halls.
  • SoftBank Sakai is 150 MW facility power; do not use the future 400 MW. SoftBank Fuchu is 35 MW IT, distinct from DayOne/Gaw Fuchu Intelligent Park (80 MW IT).
