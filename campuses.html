Named campuses, not a census.

This is a desk-curated named-project register, not a global inventory. Fifty-four pins, each folded from a primary the desk fetched. Virginia is a named-campus shelf in this book, not Data Center Alley. Ireland is a named-campus shelf, not Dublin metro. Honest holes remain: no named AWS Loudoun or AWS Prince William campus (the AWS pins here are Louisa County only), and no named AWS Ireland campus. Google’s city-only “Dublin, Ireland” line is not a pin. Sub-250 disclosed pins are kept; the 250 MW floor is a filter, not a delete. Energized MW is almost never published. A 4.75 GW interconnection is not IT. SSA, South Asia, and APAC-ex-Oceania have no pins in this book — that is a coverage hole, not a load of zero. Homer City Energy Campus is held: it is a generation plant.

How to read this

  • Not a census. Empty regions and empty small-MW filters are coverage holes, not bugs.
  • Virginia fold (2026-08-22): nineteen named campuses. This is a named-campus shelf, not Data Center Alley. No named AWS Loudoun or AWS Prince William campus; AWS pins in this book are Louisa County only.
  • Ireland fold (2026-08-22): twelve named campuses. This is a named-campus shelf, not Dublin metro. No named AWS Ireland campus. Google’s city-only “Dublin, Ireland” line is not a pin. Do not copy Meta 723.8 MW, Microsoft 1 GW / 500 MW, or Echelon 1 GW / 800 MW wind — those are generation or PPAs. Microsoft Jigginstown and Herbata share Naas townlands but are different operators; they are not merged.
  • Sub-250 disclosed pins are kept. The 250 MW floor is a filter, not a delete.
  • MW-null / not_disclosed pins stay on the default view. A MW-threshold filter hides them; they cannot pass a numeric floor.
  • Never imply a pin is live load unless status is operational and the primary said so.
  • Do not treat LBNL Queued Up as data-center load. It is generation interconnection.
  • Homer City Energy Campus is held (generation plant). It is not a pin.
  • CloudHQ LC is printed as 1,700 MW from “over 1.7 GWs”; the same page also prints 1,256 MW Total Critical IT Load. The conflict is left on the pin.
  • CloudHQ CLP has no MW: the same page prints 215 MW and 142 MW.
  • CleanArc VA1 grain is interconnection (“critical grid capacity”), not IT. EdgeCore Shannon Hill grain is facility power (“over 1.1 gigawatts of power”).
  • Vantage VA1 operational status is kept; the awards line on the location page is a weak operational quote.
  • Compass Digital Gateway is canceled (2026-04-29): “we cannot move forward with the Prince William Digital Gateway project.” No MW.
  • Google Loudoun / Prince William / Chesterfield share the VEDP 2025-08-27 primary. Do not copy Google “over 500 megawatts of new energy capacity” — generation, not campus IT.
  • Vantage DUB1 is 52 MW IT (“The first two phases consist of 52MW of IT capacity”). Do not use 100 MVA generation.
  • Herbata (Naas) is a Kildare grant that was appealed (eplanning APPEALED 16/09/2025). Status stays announced. No official MW.
  • Art Ennis inspector 200 MW is 83 grid + 120 on-site generation. MW stays null; do not display 200 as IT.
  • Red Admiral status is undisclosed (company still “seeking Planning Permission”; ABP appeal 17/06/2026). Do not invent announced.
  • CyrusOne DUB1 is 54 MW IT (“potential to provide a total 54 MW of IT capacity”). Do not use 80 MVA.
  • EdgeConneX Dublin prints 32 / 170 / 18+7+7 / 100 MW on one page. MW is left null; this desk did not pick one.
  • Mayo Data Hub / Killala is a live An Bord Pleanála appeal (322575). Status is undisclosed. Do not copy Orion / 36–60 MW.
  • Homer City Energy Campus — generation plant.
