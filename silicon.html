How the tape is ranked — and what it refuses to be

Rank = 0.40 × liquidity + 0.35 × demand + 0.25 × frontier, each scored 0–3. The composite is ordinal hygiene, not a valuation, not a market cap, not a forecast.

Liquidity. 0 none; 1 one primary venue; 2 two–three venues; 3 four or more spanning a neocloud and a hyperscaler.

Demand. 3 named sold-out, booked, or scarce in 2026 trade press; 2 listed but sales-gated or tight; 1 widely listed on-demand; 0 unknown.

Frontier. 3 current training / rack frontier; 2 current workhorse; 1 commodity / prior-gen.

Display-price convention. Prefer a labeled on-demand print from the most liquid public neocloud (Lambda, else CoreWeave, else DigitalOcean). The lead column is that buy-now print, marked OD or Spot. SemiAnalysis 1y is a dated series — last public period April 2026, STALE — and is never today's buy-now headline. A second venue stays in the row. Capacity Blocks, marketplace floors, and on-demand are never blended into one unlabeled number. TensorWave is not treated as a public list.

Term book. After the lead print: 1m (1-month / monthly lease), 1q (1-quarter), 1y (1-year / 12-month reserved, or a labeled Lambda 1-Click Cluster 2w–1y, or SA 1y), 3y (3-year reserved). Each cell is a sourced labeled $/GPU-hr. Missing tenor = em dash. We do not write 1y = OD × 0.8 or 3y = OD × 0.65.

Change windows. 1M = 30d ±5d, 1Q = 90d ±10d, 1Y = 365d ±21d, 3Y = 1095d ±45d. Percent = 100 × (now/then − 1), only from two real same-chip same-venue same-term same-config prints. A missing pair is an em dash, never an invented 0%. 1h / 24h / 7d stay dashes: US list prices do not tick daily.

Sparklines. Step charts of dated observed prints. Carry-forward is for drawing only. We do not invent 1d or 7d candles.

Tape Print. Tape Print is a same-term constellation. We do not publish the sleeve weights. The big number on the row is always the labeled venue price.

As-of dates. SemiAnalysis public prints stop at April 2026 and are labeled that way. August 2026 figures are neocloud and hyperscaler list pages fetched 2026-08-28. Snapshot is the last sourced confirm day, not the last price tick. Dated history lives in silicon-history.json (append-only). Rank snapshots live in rank-history.json (append-only observed ranks; never interpolated). Inference / Neoclouds / Hyperscalers join that tape once they have a published rank formula.

Questions the tape answers in public

What is the index?

compute.world is the world's compute & silicon index. The Compute Net Worth Index™ (CNW™) prices 108 countries. The Silicon Tape prints sourced chips. Gross Domestic Compute (GDC™) is the tapped counterpart to CNW. Two tapes, one index.

What is the tape?

The Silicon Tape is a public rental index. Every display number is a labeled term — on-demand, 1y, spot, Capacity Blocks, or token/enterprise — from a named venue and date. Rank is 0.40 × liquidity + 0.35 × demand + 0.25 × frontier. It is not a market cap.

Why is 7-day a dash?

US list prices do not tick daily. 7d lights up after a week of our own scrape. We will not invent a daily coin chart.

Why are 1m / 1q / 3y dashes on most US lists?

Venues do not publish those tenors. US neoclouds print on-demand, sometimes spot, and rarely a 12-month reserved card. They do not publish 1-month, 1-quarter, or 3-year list prices. We do not impute a discount off on-demand. A dash means no sourced tenor.

Why isn't today's H100 $2.35?

SemiAnalysis 1y $2.35 is a March 2026 print. The last public SA period is April 2026 and is labeled STALE. Buy-now is Lambda on-demand $3.99 as of 28 August 2026. The SA series still lives in the drawer, as a step chart, not as today's headline.

Why are sparklines steps, not candles?

The tape has dated observed prints — not a session market. A sparkline is a step chart of those prints. Carry-forward is for drawing only. A percent needs two real same-venue same-term prints. Missing pair = —.

What is Tape Print?

Tape Print is a same-term constellation. We do not publish the sleeve weights. The auditable number is the labeled venue price.

Why does Cerebras have no $/hour?

Cerebras Cloud is token and enterprise. There is no sourced public accelerator-hour. Aggregator ranges such as $0.75–$12.50 are omitted on purpose. Groq is the same motion: a token API, not an invented chip-hour.
