Questions people ask

How much does GH200 cost per hour?

$6.50 per GPU-hour on-demand at CoreWeave as of 8 Sep 2026 (1 GPU. No spot.). Other sourced prints: $6.50 on-demand at CoreWeave, $2.29 on-demand at Lambda.

Why do GH200 prices differ so much between clouds?

Because the terms differ. On-demand is a list price with no commitment; spot is interruptible and priced to fill idle capacity; reserved and one-year contracts trade a commitment for a lower rate; AWS Capacity Blocks are scheduled reservations, not on-demand. The tape never averages these: each print carries its venue, term and date.
