India sits on a resource ceiling of roughly 271 GW, worth $16.3T to $21.7T of AI compute at today's prices, about 6 times its $3.9T GDP. The bankable slice today is $1.7T, which is $1.2k for every citizen, discounted mainly for physical and governance. It has built 20.3% of the ceiling and runs 1.9 GW on a grid of roughly 470 GW, a Gross Domestic Compute of $95B. Primed: readiness clears 65 percent, and there is real headroom.
