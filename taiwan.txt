Taiwan sits on a resource ceiling of roughly 16 GW, worth $943B to $1.3T of AI compute at today's prices, about 1 times its $920B GDP. The bankable slice today is $153B, which is $6.7k for every citizen, discounted mainly for physical and stability. It has built 29.9% of the ceiling and runs about 450 MW on a grid of roughly 65 GW, a Gross Domestic Compute of $22B. An incumbent: already priced in, already building.
