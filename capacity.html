How this page sits on the desk

Desk-curated company capacity book. Figures are extracted from SEC EDGAR and official IR/press only. Not scraped from neocloudstocks.com. Live + contracted + construction + pipeline is not additive with secured power. Portfolio totals on the page sum only status in {reported, calculated} and exclude estimated. No EV/MW or Revenue/MW in this edition. GPU cells stay undisclosed unless the issuer printed a fleet total.

  • Issuer book: company filings. Campus panel: project pins. Do not add the two columns and call it a fleet.
  • Prefer critical IT / AI load when the issuer distinguishes it from bitcoin nameplate. When they do not, the power layer stays on the cell.
  • No GPU density fill. No EV/MW. No scrape of a third-party capacity explorer.
