Jordan: a narrower gap, and a real one

Jordan sits on a resource ceiling of roughly 14 GW, worth $868B to $1.2T of AI compute at today's prices, about 19 times its $62B GDP. The bankable slice today is $144B, which is $12.0k for every citizen, discounted mainly for capital and governance. It has built 0.0% of the ceiling and runs about 30 MW on a grid of roughly 7 GW, a Gross Domestic Compute of $1.5B. Emerging upside: a narrower gap, and a real one.
