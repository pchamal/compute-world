Compute brief - 30 October 2026, as printed.

Amazon closed out hyperscaler earnings week with the same message as Google and Microsoft: demand is ahead of supply. AWS revenue rose 39.3% to $46.0 billion, its fastest growth in nearly five years, its backlog reached $578 billion, more than double a year earlier, and Andy Jassy said AWS is installing an AI accelerator about every 14 seconds with customers taking it almost as fast. Cash capex was $75.9 billion in the quarter; Amazon kept 2026 at about $220 billion and said 2027 will be meaningfully more. Upstream, the squeeze is in packaging: Intel said substrate supply limits how fast it can scale EMIB, and Taiwan press reports TSMC is weighing CoWoS price rises of 10% to 20%. Ottawa turned sovereign AI into a compute obligation, with up to C$1.6 billion of equity in Cohere tied to at least C$1.7 billion of new Canadian computing infrastructure by 2030. On the public tape all 70 list prints confirmed; Lambda H100 holds at $3.99 and B200 at $6.69 per GPU-hour.

In brief

What is the index?

compute.world is the world's compute & silicon index: CNW™ prices 108 countries; the Silicon Tape prints sourced chips.

What is the tape?

This page is the weekday public brief — country conversion already on The Wire, plus labeled silicon display prints. Not a market cap.

Why is 7-day a dash?

US list prices do not tick daily. 7d lights up after a week of our own scrape. H100 1Y (+33.4%) is Lambda $2.99 → $3.99, not a daily candle. SA 1y $2.35 is STALE.

Why are 1m / 1q / 3y dashes on most US lists?

Venues do not publish those tenors. We do not impute a discount off on-demand.

Why does Cerebras have no $/hour?

Token / enterprise. No public accelerator-hour. We will not print aggregator $0.75–$12.50 as a Cerebras hour.
