The daily tape, as printed.

Two 350-plus-megawatt conversions landed inside 72 hours, and both run through somebody else's balance sheet: Fervo signed a 396 MW, 15-year PPA with Google for Cape Station in Beaver County, Utah — four 99 MW tranches from Q3 2028, plus an option on ~600 MW more by June 2030 — while WSJ, Bloomberg and Reuters report Anthropic taking ~350 MW at Hut 8's Beacon Point through a $35B Lambda contract on which Nvidia holds the lease. Sovereign buyers moved the same week: EuroHPC signed €387.8M with Bull for LUMI-AI on AMD MI430X in Kajaani, and Korea's cabinet sent the National Assembly a 2027 budget carrying ₩21.3T for AI and three megaprojects, including ₩3.85T to add 10,000 Vera Rubin-class GPUs. Silicon Tape: clean confirm day — 65 of 65 rows re-read unchanged across six venues (Lambda H100 $3.99 / B200 $6.69), with the H100 1Y window going dark as the 12 Aug 2025 $2.99 print aged past the ±21-day tolerance.

Countries

  • United States — Fervo Energy (Nasdaq: FRVO), 1 Sep: 396 MW, 15-year PPA with Google for the Cape Station EGS GeoCluster in Beaver County, Utah. The 8-K dates the contract 26 Aug between Cape Generating Station 6 LLC and Google Energy LLC — four successive 99 MW sub-tranches, target COD from Q3 2028, parent guarantees from Fervo and Alphabet, plus an offered option on ~600 MW more (≈950 MW total) with a guaranteed COD no later than June 2030. Google calls the energy a foundational building block for a potential Utah data center; delivery leans on SB 132 direct-to-load pathways. Wire today.
  • United States — Reported 31 Aug (WSJ first, matched by Bloomberg and Reuters): a ~$35B, six-year Anthropic–Lambda cloud contract covering roughly 350 MW at Hut 8's Beacon Point campus in Nueces County, Texas, with Nvidia holding the underlying lease and supplying the GPUs. Hut 8 disclosed in July two 15-year leases totalling ~704 MW at $19.6B base-term value to an unnamed investment-grade tenant. Initial energization expected Q1 2027. No party has confirmed the contract. Wire today, flagged unconfirmed.
  • Finland — EuroHPC JU, 31 Aug: procurement contract signed with Bull for LUMI-AI at CSC's Kajaani data centre — €387,800,000 total, funded 50% by EuroHPC (Digital Europe Programme) and 50% by the LUMI AI Factory consortium (Finland, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Norway, Poland). Built on AMD Instinct MI430X GPUs and 6th Gen 256-core EPYC, targeted at ~10× LUMI's current AI capacity, installed and open to users in 2027. Sixth EuroHPC AI Factory contract. Wire today.
  • South Korea — Cabinet approved 1 Sep: a record ₩820.9T 2027 budget (+12.8%) in which AI plus the three megaprojects — semiconductors, physical AI, AI data centres — nearly double from ₩10.8T to ₩21.3T. MSIT's own budget is ₩29.65T with AI up 84.3% to ₩9.4T, including ₩3.85T (from ₩2.08T) to add 10,000 Nvidia Vera Rubin-class GPUs for sovereign models, a 10 MW AIDC testbed and ₩87.8B for an AIDC cluster. Submitted to the National Assembly 3 Sep; passage required by 2 Dec. Wire today.
  • Saudi Arabia — AMD, Cisco and PIF-owned HUMAIN, 31 Aug: MI355X-based infrastructure on Cisco Silicon One networking and 800G optics is live and serving customers, the JV's first production milestone. Next phase is up to 250 MW on MI400-series beginning 2027 with capacity from H2 2027; the JV still targets up to 1 GW by 2030. No megawatts, customers or pricing disclosed for the live system, and the original phase-1 plan named 100 MW of MI450. Wire today at tier 2.
  • Malaysia — DayOne and TNB GenCo disclosed 1 Sep a 7 Aug non-binding MOU to study up to 1.5 GW of dedicated on-site generation plus storage for DayOne's planned Greater Kuala Lumpur campus in Selangor. Capacity and configuration remain subject to feasibility work, definitive agreements and regulatory approval. HOLD off Wire until something binding exists.

In brief

What is the index?

compute.world is the world's compute & silicon index: CNW™ prices 108 countries; the Silicon Tape prints sourced chips.

What is the tape?

This page is the weekday public brief — country conversion already on The Wire, plus labeled silicon display prints. Not a market cap.

Why is 7-day a dash?

US list prices do not tick daily. 7d lights up after a week of our own scrape. H100 1Y (+33.4%) is Lambda $2.99 → $3.99, not a daily candle. SA 1y $2.35 is STALE.

Why are 1m / 1q / 3y dashes on most US lists?

Venues do not publish those tenors. We do not impute a discount off on-demand.

Why does Cerebras have no $/hour?

Token / enterprise. No public accelerator-hour. We will not print aggregator $0.75–$12.50 as a Cerebras hour.
