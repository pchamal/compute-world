Compute brief - 20 October 2026, as printed.

Two answers to the same grid constraint landed between Sunday and Monday. In Arkansas the tenant pays: Entergy Arkansas announced a second phase of service for Google's West Memphis campus, with Google covering about $600 million of battery, substation and transmission investment plus a 440 MW solar PPA, pending Arkansas PSC approval and an expected 2028 in-service date. Where no tenant pays, governments ration: Denmark's executive order 892 took effect on 19 October and places data centres in the lowest of four priority categories when grid capacity is short, and Israel's Electricity Authority backed a 200 MW cap per new data center, about 1,000 MW of new large connections and NIS 2 million per MW guarantees after 27,000 MW of requests. Goldman Sachs counts 86 data center projects canceled or significantly delayed since January, 76 of them tied to local opposition, and calls power 'probably the forever bottleneck'. Capital is still chasing sites with power: Bloomberg and CNBC report Anthropic is in talks for a second 460 MW building at Nscale's Monarch campus in West Virginia worth about $45 billion, even as investors push back on Nscale's roughly $30 billion IPO target and Korea's Elice fell about 30% on its KOSDAQ debut. On the public silicon tape all six venues returned HTTP 200: 71 confirms, 0 ticks, 0 NEW. CoreWeave's list held at $6.16 for H100 even as Goldman's upgrade note cites CoreWeave price increases of 25% in July and 10% in October, which sit in contracts, not the public page.

In brief

What is the index?

compute.world is the world's compute & silicon index: CNW™ prices 108 countries; the Silicon Tape prints sourced chips.

What is the tape?

This page is the weekday public brief — country conversion already on The Wire, plus labeled silicon display prints. Not a market cap.

Why is 7-day a dash?

US list prices do not tick daily. 7d lights up after a week of our own scrape. H100 1Y (+33.4%) is Lambda $2.99 → $3.99, not a daily candle. SA 1y $2.35 is STALE.

Why are 1m / 1q / 3y dashes on most US lists?

Venues do not publish those tenors. We do not impute a discount off on-demand.

Why does Cerebras have no $/hour?

Token / enterprise. No public accelerator-hour. We will not print aggregator $0.75–$12.50 as a Cerebras hour.
