Compute brief — 4 September 2026, as printed.

The binding constraint on US AI campuses this week is not another GPU list print — Lambda H100 is still $3.99 and B200 $6.69 on a clean confirm day — it is who is allowed to sell the transformers and inverters that hook those racks to the grid. Executive Order 14420 (26 Aug) puts foreign-produced bulk-power equipment under DOE emergency authority, with implementing rules due in 120 days; CNBC and Wood Mackenzie on 2–3 Sep spell out why that lands on data centers first. Crusoe’s reported $3B round at a $30B valuation (TechCrunch/Bloomberg, 3 Sep) shows neocloud balance sheets still clearing, but Howard–Solstice 59336 remains without a final CCN order.

Countries

  • United States — EO 14420 (26 Aug) authorizes DOE to restrict foreign-produced BPS equipment (transformers, inverters, BESS, UPS, controls). Rules due ~120 days; CNBC 3 Sep frames China share in DC power stack as the exposure.
  • China — Covered Foreign Entity pathway plus reported China share (~30% some transformer/switchgear categories; >40% US battery imports per Johns Hopkins quotes in CNBC) is the geopolitical hinge of the order.
  • United States / Texas — PUCT 59336 Howard–Solstice still HOLD: Interchange shows Commission Counsel memorandum + protest filings 3 Sep, no final order after Order No. 1 reopened the record on 28 Aug.

In brief

What is the index?

compute.world is the world's compute & silicon index: CNW™ prices 108 countries; the Silicon Tape prints sourced chips.

What is the tape?

This page is the weekday public brief — country conversion already on The Wire, plus labeled silicon display prints. Not a market cap.

Why is 7-day a dash?

US list prices do not tick daily. 7d lights up after a week of our own scrape. H100 1Y (+33.4%) is Lambda $2.99 → $3.99, not a daily candle. SA 1y $2.35 is STALE.

Why are 1m / 1q / 3y dashes on most US lists?

Venues do not publish those tenors. We do not impute a discount off on-demand.

Why does Cerebras have no $/hour?

Token / enterprise. No public accelerator-hour. We will not print aggregator $0.75–$12.50 as a Cerebras hour.
