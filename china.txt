China sits on a resource ceiling of roughly 744 GW, worth $44.7T to $59.6T of AI compute at today's prices, about 3 times its $19.6T GDP. The bankable slice today is $2.7T, which is $1.9k for every citizen, discounted mainly for chip access and governance. It has built 50.6% of the ceiling and runs 23.0 GW on a grid of roughly 3350 GW, a Gross Domestic Compute of $1.1T. Primed: readiness clears 65 percent, and there is real headroom.
